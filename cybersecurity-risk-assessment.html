Small-Business Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

A cybersecurity risk assessment of a simulated small-business environment, identifying weaknesses across network security, endpoint protection, authentication, remote access, resilience, and employee security awareness, then translating those findings into practical security recommendations.

Risk Assessment Risk Management Security Controls Threat Analysis Incident Response Business Continuity Security Awareness

Project Overview

TechEase Solutions was a simulated 15-employee small business used to evaluate how common technology and process weaknesses can translate into cybersecurity risk. The assessment examined the organization's existing environment, identified vulnerabilities and associated threats, evaluated their potential consequences, and developed recommendations intended to reduce exposure and improve security maturity.

Threat Landscape

The assessment considered both external and internal sources of risk. Understanding who might target the organization helped connect technical weaknesses to realistic business threats.

THREAT 01

Cybercriminals

External attackers could exploit weak network security, compromised credentials, vulnerable endpoints, or remote-access weaknesses for financial gain or unauthorized access.

THREAT 02

Insider Threats

Employees or other trusted users could intentionally or accidentally expose systems and information through misuse, mistakes, or poor security practices.

THREAT 03

Script Kiddies

Less sophisticated attackers using readily available tools could still take advantage of weak configurations or exposed systems.

THREAT 04

Social Engineering

Limited employee security training increased exposure to phishing and other techniques designed to manipulate users into revealing information or granting access.

Risk Analysis

Each weakness was connected to the security risk it created and a practical recommendation designed to reduce that exposure.

RISK 01 — NETWORK PERIMETER

Basic Router

Weakness

The organization relied on a basic router that provided limited enterprise security capabilities.

Risk

Insufficient perimeter protection could increase the likelihood of unauthorized network access.

Recommended Control

Deploy a business-grade router, maintain firmware updates, and use secure VPN access for remote connectivity.

RISK 02 — NETWORK SEGMENTATION

Shared Guest & Employee Wi-Fi

Weakness

Employees and guests shared network access without sufficient separation.

Risk

A compromised or untrusted device could increase exposure to internal systems and enable unnecessary lateral network access.

Recommended Control

Separate guest and employee traffic through network segmentation and restrict guest access to internal resources.

RISK 03 — REMOTE ACCESS

Unvetted Remote-Access Tools

Weakness

Remote workers relied on third-party remote-access tools without a standardized security review or approved access method.

Risk

Uncontrolled third-party access could expose credentials, systems, or business information to unauthorized access or vendor-related risk.

Recommended Control

Establish an approved remote-access solution using secure VPN connectivity and controlled authentication requirements.

RISK 04 — ENDPOINT SECURITY

No Endpoint Protection

Weakness

Company endpoints lacked a standardized endpoint detection and protection capability.

Risk

Malware or ransomware could execute without sufficient detection, potentially affecting systems and business data.

Recommended Control

Deploy endpoint detection and response protection across company devices and maintain consistent security configurations.

RISK 05 — AUTHENTICATION

Weak Password Practices

Weakness

Minimal password requirements increased the likelihood of weak, reused, or easily compromised credentials.

Risk

Credential compromise could allow attackers to impersonate legitimate users and access organizational resources.

Recommended Control

Establish stronger password requirements and a formal password management policy.

RISK 06 — RESILIENCE

No Incident Response or Backup Plan

Weakness

The organization lacked formal incident-response and backup/recovery procedures.

Risk

A security incident could result in extended downtime, data loss, and an uncoordinated response.

Recommended Control

Develop an incident-response plan, disaster-recovery procedures, and a documented backup strategy.

RISK 07 — HUMAN RISK

Limited Security Awareness Training

Weakness

Employees lacked formal cybersecurity awareness training.

Risk

Users could be more susceptible to phishing, social engineering, and other attacks that target human behavior.

Recommended Control

Implement recurring security-awareness education focused on phishing, social engineering, credential security, and safe computing practices.

Control Strategy

The recommendations were designed as layers rather than isolated fixes. Together, they addressed technical, procedural, and human sources of cybersecurity risk.

CONTROL AREA 01

Preventive Controls

Stronger authentication, network segmentation, secure remote access, and improved perimeter security reduce opportunities for unauthorized access.

CONTROL AREA 02

Endpoint Protection

Standardized endpoint security improves the organization's ability to prevent and detect malware or other malicious activity.

CONTROL AREA 03

Response & Recovery

Incident-response, backup, and recovery planning reduce operational disruption and improve the organization's ability to recover from a security event.

CONTROL AREA 04

People & Process

Security-awareness training and documented security practices address risks that cannot be reduced through technical controls alone.

Risk Management Perspective

The project demonstrated that cybersecurity risk assessment is not simply the identification of technical vulnerabilities. Effective risk analysis connects a weakness to the threat it creates, considers how that risk could affect the organization, and recommends controls that are appropriate for the business environment.

Portfolio Note

This case study is based on a simulated small-business environment created as part of cybersecurity training. It contains no confidential client, employer, or proprietary information.
