Investigation & Risk Assessment

Investigations used open-source intelligence and cybersecurity techniques to evaluate an individual's or organization's publicly accessible digital footprint and identify information that could increase privacy, cyber, or physical-security risk.

INVESTIGATION AREA 01

PII Exposure

Identified exposed personal information across publicly accessible sources and evaluated how individual data points could contribute to a broader exposure profile.

INVESTIGATION AREA 02

Data Brokers & People-Finder Sites

Investigated personal information appearing across data-broker and people-finder platforms and performed manual removal and validation activities where required.

INVESTIGATION AREA 03

Public Web & Social Exposure

Reviewed publicly accessible web sources, social-media exposure, public records, and other discoverable information that could expand an individual's digital attack surface.

INVESTIGATION AREA 04

Human Perimeter Risk

Considered how information involving family members, relationships, photographs, locations, and other contextual details could contribute to social-engineering, privacy, or physical-security exposure.

02

Removal

Continued monitoring for newly exposed or reappearing information, recognizing that digital privacy protection requires an ongoing lifecycle rather than a one-time removal.
